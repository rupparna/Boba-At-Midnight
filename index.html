What happened to the 13th drink?👀

Ube Fog used to be our best seller. Purple, cold, a little smoky on top — regulars swore it tasted different every time, like it knew what kind of night you were having. It was the only drink on the menu that came with no ice option, because the owner said "it's already cold enough where it's from."

One night a customer ordered it at exactly 11:59 PM, paid in exact change, and sat down by the window. The barista turned around to grab a straw. When she turned back, the cup was still full, still steaming a little, and the seat was empty. The door hadn't opened. The bell never rang.

We pulled Ube Fog off the menu the next morning. Nobody talks about why the cashier quit that same week. But sometimes, right around midnight, someone taps twice on the counter and asks for "the usual" — and whoever's working that night just says we're out.
